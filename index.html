Knee Care Protocol

Her knee is the #1 priority. Every exercise is designed to protect it while building the muscles that support it — these are not in conflict.

🚨 Non-Negotiable Rules

🚫 Never fully lock or hyperextend the knee during any move
⚠️ Stop squats at 90° — no deep squats until the knee is stronger
🧊 Ice the knee 10 min after workout if any discomfort arises
🛑 Pain = Stop. Do NOT push through knee pain — ever
🩺 If pain persists beyond 2 weeks, see a physiotherapist
💊 Continue multivitamins — add Vitamin D3 & Omega-3 if possible

✅ Why This Plan Helps the Knee

Glute BridgesStrong glutes take load directly OFF the knee joint — the single most impactful move for knee pain.
ClamshellsHip abductor strength prevents the knee from collapsing inward — a primary cause of chronic pain.
Calf RaisesStrong calves absorb shock during movement, reducing stress on the knee dramatically.
Pilates CoreA strong core stabilises the full body, including how forces travel through the knee on every step.
Hip Flexor StretchesTight hip flexors directly pull on the kneecap — stretching them is immediate pain relief.
Active Recovery WalksGentle movement pumps synovial fluid into knee cartilage. Better than complete rest.

💊 Supplement Suggestions

She already takes multivitamins — great foundation. Consider adding:

Vitamin D3 + K2Critical for bone density, immune function & joint health. Most people are deficient.
Omega-3 (Fish Oil)Potent natural anti-inflammatory. Directly reduces joint pain and swelling over time.
Collagen PeptidesRebuilds cartilage. Best taken with Vitamin C for optimal absorption.
Magnesium GlycinateReduces muscle soreness and night cramps — especially useful for calves and legs.

* Always confirm new supplements with a doctor.

8-Week Progression

The plan builds slowly — because she's reluctant, and that's completely okay. Small wins compound into lasting transformation.

Week 1–2
Form & Habit
Focus entirely on correct form, not intensity. Three sessions will feel hard — that's completely normal and expected.
Week 3–4
Add Reps
Add 2–3 reps to each set where it feels easy. Plank holds extend by 10 seconds. Knee should feel noticeably more stable.
Week 5–6
Add Sets
Add a 4th set to your favourite exercises. Introduce resistance bands if purchased — complete game changer for legs and glutes.
Week 7–8
Full Intensity
Circuits feel manageable. Push-ups progress toward full (not on knees). Visible strength gains. Celebrate every win — they're real.

🌟 For the Reluctant Starter

The first two weeks will feel hard. That's NOT a sign it isn't working — it means it is.
She doesn't need to feel motivated to start. She just needs to start. Motivation follows action, always.
Missing one day is human. Missing three in a row is a pattern. Get back on day two — always.
Track one simple thing: did I do it today? Yes or no. No calorie counting, no weight obsession needed.
Her knee will get STRONGER with this plan — not worse. Pain-free, capable movement is the real goal.
